Alpha.21's fix (the sim pulses before the frame reads it) left the local warlock juddering under
the follow camera: the renderer read `performance.now()` after the frame's pulse, so a frame that
stepped a tick read milliseconds later than one that did not. The clock's frame pulses, the
interpolation and the camera's dt now all use the frame's timestamp (`document.timeline`,
`73f749c`). Measured in headless Chrome (solo, 9 bots, the local warlock walking, 60 Hz): the
frame-to-frame swing of the interpolation time went from 2.8 ms median and 7.5 ms p90 (Chrome
149; 0.7 / 1.5 ms in Chrome 156) to 0, and the warlock's per-frame speed variation from 29% to 0
(median). Both regions keep 30 Hz; the protocol stays 10. The netcode id moved because
`src/net/clock.ts` changed (no wire or sim rule change), so open alpha.21 tabs must reload to join.
