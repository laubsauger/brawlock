A browser battle arena by Promp' Industries

Spellcraft. Knockback. Lava.

Brawlock is a free multiplayer game where warlocks battle on a shrinking arena. Aim your spells, dodge incoming attacks and knock your opponents into the lava. The last warlock standing wins the round.

Play Brawlock

Pre-alpha · Play on a desktop browser with a mouse and keyboard.

Choose your battle

  • Casual: jump into a public game. Bots fill empty seats, and guests can play without signing in.
  • Ranked: sign in to compete for global Elo and keep your account across computers.
  • Custom games: choose your rules, practise against bots or invite friends with a room code.

Your Brawlock account

Sign in with Google to create an account, choose a player name and keep your ranked rating and cosmetic progression across devices. Google sign-in requests only basic identity information: your name, email address and profile. It does not request access to Gmail, Drive, Calendar or your contacts. You can also add a passkey after creating an account.

Your chosen player name and gameplay record can be visible to other players; your account email is private. Read the Privacy Policy for the data we use and the Terms of Service for the rules of play.

About this project

Brawlock is an independent game by Promp' Industries. It is free to play; voluntary donations support its servers and development. It is inspired by the Warlock custom games for Warcraft III and Dota 2. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment or Valve.

For support, privacy or account requests, use our private contact form.
