Audition a few directions for each part of the arena. Mark what you like, leave a note, then export your choices so we can try the strongest sounds in the game.

Your choices save in this browser. Export to share them.

Current sounds are snapshots of the game's complete sound recipes. Candidates are individual source recordings to consider for layering or replacement. All previews are peak-matched to −6 dBFS for this review; final game loudness will be set separately. New candidates are indexed as CC0 by Openverse. Use their source links to inspect the originals.
